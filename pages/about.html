Our story

Our Story

Green Valley Dairy Farm was established with a simple goal: to build a responsible dairy farm focused on quality, sustainability and long-term value for Uganda’s agricultural sector.

Who we are

Built on a Vision for Better Dairy Farming

Green Valley Dairy Farm is a Ugandan dairy farming enterprise committed to quality milk production, responsible livestock management and sustainable agriculture.

We started in 2018 with a small herd and a vision to build a professionally managed dairy farm capable of supplying quality milk while contributing to Uganda’s agricultural sector.
